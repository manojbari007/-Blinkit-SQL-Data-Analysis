# ⚔️ Business Challenge

Blinkit processes **millions of orders every year**.

Looking at **3.8 Million rows manually** doesn't answer important business questions like:

- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- Which stores perform the best?
- Why are customers cancelling orders?
- Which payment methods are growing?
- When does Blinkit receive maximum orders?
- How efficient are deliveries?

Our mission was to answer these questions using SQL.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

🏆 UPI is the most preferred payment method.

🏆 Evening hours receive maximum orders.

🏆 October & November show festive sales spikes.

🏆 Less than 2% cancellation rate indicates strong operational efficiency.

🏆 Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru contribute the highest revenue.

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Improve inventory planning.
- Increase delivery partners during peak hours.
- Promote digital payments.
- Reward loyal customers.
- Optimize discount strategies.
- Expand Express Delivery.
